2. Provision of the website and app

When PointerScore is accessed, technically necessary access data may be processed. This may include IP address, time of access, requested file, referrer, browser type, operating system and technical connection data. Processing is carried out to provide PointerScore securely and reliably.

3. Registration, login and account functions

PointerScore uses Supabase Auth for registration, login, session management, logout, email confirmation and password reset. The provider is Supabase, Inc., USA. In particular, email address, encrypted password, user ID, authentication and session data, login times and technically necessary connection data may be processed.

4. Email confirmation and password reset

Automated emails may be sent to the email address provided for account confirmation and password reset. The links in these emails are used only to confirm or restore the account. Email delivery may be handled through Supabase and configured email providers such as Resend.

5. Analysis data

Company names, entered financial metrics, calculated PointerScore results, notes and related analysis data may be stored in Supabase and linked to the logged-in user account. Access is intended to be restricted to the respective user.

6. Local storage

Technically necessary authentication and session tokens may be stored locally so that login sessions remain active and can be securely refreshed. The selected language and necessary app preferences may also be stored locally.

7. Contact

If you contact PointerScore by email or through a support function, the information provided will be processed to handle the request.

8. Storage period and deletion

Account and analysis data is stored as long as the user account exists or statutory retention obligations apply. Deletion of an account or privacy-related requests can be sent to pointerscore@outlook.com.

9. Rights of data subjects

Data subjects may have rights to access, rectification, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the applicable legal requirements. There may also be a right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
