2. Website, hosting and connection data

The website is served through GitHub Pages. Servers involved process technical data, particularly IP address, requested resource, time, browser and operating system information and, where transmitted, referrer. This supports delivery, security and troubleshooting. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, reflecting our interest in secure and reliable operation. GitHub hosting information: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

3. Account, authentication and Supabase

Supabase is used for registration, email confirmation, login, session management and password reset. This involves email address, user ID, password hash, authentication and session data and technical logs. Your password is not stored as readable plain text in your profile. Account functions cannot be provided without the necessary login data. The information website can be used without an account. Account and analysis processing described here concerns the apps and technical email callback pages, not ordinary visits to the information website. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; security measures also rely on Article 6(1)(f). The provider is Supabase Pte. Ltd.; information: https://supabase.com/privacy and https://supabase.com/legal/dpa

4. Analyses, notes and profile

Saved company names, financial metrics, scores, notes and timestamps are linked to your user ID in Supabase so they are available across your devices. Display name, username and profile image are optional profile details. Analyses are intended for account-based access and are not published as public community posts. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Avoid unnecessary personal or particularly confidential information in notes. PDF reports contain the selected analysis data and, where included, your notes; you control the sharing of those files.

5. Profile images

Uploaded profile images are stored in Supabase Storage. The current implementation uses publicly accessible image URLs. Anyone who knows such a URL can retrieve the image without logging in. Only upload images you intend to make accessible in this way. Images are processed to provide the profile display you request, under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. To request removal of separately stored image files, contact pointerscore@outlook.com.

6. Account emails and Resend

Confirmation, recovery and account-related welcome emails are sent through authentication and the configured email provider Resend. Processing includes recipient address, message content including necessary confirmation links and technical delivery information. This supports account activation, security and the requested service, under Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. Registration is not a newsletter subscription. Resend is operated by Plus Five Five, Inc.: https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy and https://resend.com/legal/dpa

7. Local storage and app preferences

The browser or desktop app stores login tokens, your selected language and functional settings such as dismissed app notices. Analyses may also be held in a user-specific local cache, separate from cloud storage. This supports requested account, recovery and interface functions. Necessary device access is based on section 25(2)(2) TDDDG; subsequent processing relies on Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR depending on the function. Local preferences and analysis copies may remain until site data is cleared. Logging out does not automatically remove all local data. On shared devices, clear site data after use.

8. Company data: SEC EDGAR and Stooq

The data helper runs only when you request it and requires login. Your ticker and the authentication information needed to check access are sent to a Supabase Edge Function. It retrieves company data from the US Securities and Exchange Commission and, where available, prices from Stooq. These sources receive the company request and technical server connection data, not your saved analyses, notes or account access tokens. Values are imported into an analysis only after your selection. The requested function is processed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

9. Local fonts and external program libraries

The updated desktop app includes Inter font files and the required Supabase library locally in its installation package. The information website also serves fonts itself; the technical email verification page uses a Supabase library shipped with the website. No Google Fonts or jsDelivr requests are needed for these resources. Older desktop versions may still load the Supabase library from jsDelivr until updated. Such requests involve technical connection data, including IP address and requested resource; their purpose is to provide the requested account features under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Information about the previous CDN: https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy

10. Contact, support and external links

When you email us, we process the sender address, message and any voluntarily attached files to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for account matters, otherwise Article 6(1)(f) for responding. The support function opens your email application and does not independently send a message. Our contact address is an Outlook mailbox, so Microsoft is also involved in email delivery. Information: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

When you choose to open external links, such as the Microsoft Store or data sources, the relevant provider’s privacy information also applies.

11. Recipients and international processing

The hosting, authentication, storage, email and CDN services named above receive data to the described extent. International providers and subprocessors may process data outside the EU or EEA. Such transfers must meet Articles 44 et seq. GDPR, in particular through an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request information about the recipients and safeguards applicable to your data at pointerscore@outlook.com. We do not promise exclusively European processing across all services.

12. Retention and deletion

Account, profile and analysis data is stored for the duration of the requested use. Individual analyses can be deleted in the dashboard. Account deletion can be initiated in the profile or requested by email. This removes the account and linked profile and analysis records; separately stored images, already generated reports and local copies are distinct. You may additionally request deletion of image files and other remaining personal data. Technical logs and support messages are retained only as required for operation, security, handling enquiries, legal duties or resolving claims. Technical backups follow their applicable deletion cycles. You can remove your downloaded PDFs and local site data yourself.

13. Your rights

Subject to the legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability under Articles 15–20 GDPR. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent for the future. You may object to processing under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation, as provided by Article 21. Contact pointerscore@outlook.com. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony: https://www.lfd.niedersachsen.de

14. No advertising tracking or personal credit decisions

The current website code does not include analytics or marketing trackers such as Google Analytics or advertising pixels. User data is not sold for advertising profiles. PointerScore evaluates user-selected companies using financial metrics. It does not make an automated decision about you with legal or similarly significant effects under Article 22 GDPR. This notice is updated when the functions actually used change.

15. Local storage and no external Google Fonts

The information website serves fonts locally from its own server and contains no Google Fonts requests, advertising tracker or embedded store checkout. Your language choice is stored locally in the browser to retain the presentation you selected. External store or help pages open only when you click their links. The app area may additionally store necessary session data, drafts and analysis data locally. External services described above are used only in their respective feature context, not by merely visiting the homepage.

Such processing serves the selected contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for required records, legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR). No Google Play billing takes place on this information website. Further app-specific privacy information will be provided when the mobile app is published. This notice describes the currently available information website and existing desktop app, and the planned billing model.
